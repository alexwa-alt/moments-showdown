📖 Formula help
  • moment (N m) = force (N) × perpendicular distance from the pivot (m)
  • Principle of moments: when balanced, total clockwise moment = total anticlockwise moment
  • Weight acts through the centre of gravity (CoG)
  • An object topples if the vertical line down from its CoG falls outside its base
  • 100 cm = 1 m. Convert before you multiply!
